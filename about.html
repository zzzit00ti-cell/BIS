About our school

Center for holistic quality education

Bonafide International School (BIS) is a learning community in Hawassa, Sidama, where every student is empowered to thrive in a dynamic world.

Our vision is to cultivate well-rounded individuals who excel in technology, maintain optimal physical and mental well-being, embrace cultural diversity and demonstrate strong social skills.

  • ✔ Cultivating well-rounded individuals.
  • ✔ Excelling in technology.
  • ✔ Promoting physical and mental well-being.
  • ✔ Embracing cultural diversity.
  • ✔ Developing strong social skills.

What we focus on

Academics

A structured curriculum with continuous assessment so every learner knows exactly where they stand.

Technology

ICT and computing labs that build coding and digital literacy from an early age.

Wellbeing

Sports, art and music alongside a school clinic and dedicated pastoral support.

Community

Strong social skills, cultural awareness and a safe, friendly campus for every family.
